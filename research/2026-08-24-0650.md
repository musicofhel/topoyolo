## Findings / surprises
- Header format is far less uniform than METHODOLOGY implies: two dash styles
  (`---` vs `—`), three hash levels (`##` and `###` paper headers), ids that are
  arXiv-new / arXiv-old / bare DOIs / publisher slugs (`s42005-021-00605-4`,
  `eLife.03476`). The lint accepts all of these; A2/A3 should normalize toward
  one grammar rather than grow more regexes.
- The "dual index" is not purely an index: by-domain files contain many full
  annotations inline. Any future count-based invariant ("N papers") needs A4 to
  derive from one canonical place first.
- Markdown-link check must ignore prose like `K[g](w)`; enforced by requiring
  path-like targets.
