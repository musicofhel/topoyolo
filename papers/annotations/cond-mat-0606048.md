## cond-mat/0606048 --- Oh, Lee, Kahng, Kim (2006)
**"Synchronization transition of heterogeneously coupled oscillators on scale-free networks"**
arXiv: cond-mat/0606048 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: Finite-size scaling of the order parameter derived for each regime — the critical exponents quantify how the synchronization transition's scaling behavior is stable under the perturbation "make N finite," and J_c itself is shown stable (finite) or unstable (zero) depending on where (η, λ) sits relative to the λ−2 line.
- **Parameterized homology**: The paper's real product is a *regime taxonomy*: eight distinct transition behaviors indexed by two parameters (coupling-degree exponent η, network degree exponent λ), with the order-parameter class changing as those parameters cross critical lines — tracking how an invariant of the coupled system (its transition class) varies along a parameter sweep. Moderate machine: the tracked object is a critical-behavior class, not a topological invariant.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess** (weak): the cluster-formation view treats synchronized clusters as emergent composites; cluster-size distributions and largest-cluster scaling are composite-level quantities not recoverable from single-oscillator properties.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Degree-dependent asymmetric coupling J k_i^{η−1} as a one-parameter dial that interpolates between vanishing and finite J_c on scale-free networks — the η = λ−2 threshold line organizing all eight behaviors is the paper's structural contribution.
- Generating-function derivation of cluster-size distributions per regime: the sync transition re-derived as a percolation-like clustering process.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: scale-free network literature, mean-field Kuramoto theory, generating-function techniques (Newman lineage), percolation analogy via cluster formation. No TDA-as-tool, QEC, OT.
