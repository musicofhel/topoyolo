## 2201.06650 --- Gulen & McCleary (2022)
**"Galois Connections in Persistent Homology"**
arXiv: 2201.06650 | math.AT

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: PH is re-expressed in the language of Galois connections between posets — the persistence-module setup and its diagrams are recast so that interleavings, matchings, and multiparameter persistence diagrams become instances of one adjoint-pair formalism.

- **Matching**: Matchings on barcodes are unified with interleavings via Galois connections; Rota's Galois connection theorem then does work normally done by matching arguments directly.

- **Stability**: The headline application: Rota's theorem yields a *substantially easier proof of the bottleneck stability theorem*. Stability results drop out of an order-theoretic theorem rather than from combinatorial matching estimates — a second, independent route to the same robustness guarantee.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Unification by adjunction: interleavings AND matchings shown to be two faces of one Galois-connection structure — for this atlas, two of the six machines revealed as order-theoretically dual in the PH setting.
2. Proof-technology transfer: a classical combinatorics/order-theory theorem (Rota) imported as the engine of a stability proof — the same "import mature machinery as proof technology" pattern seen elsewhere in the atlas (e.g. OT imported into BP analysis).
3. Multiparameter relationships: various multiparameter persistence diagram notions related within the new language — groundwork where the atlas's single-parameter PH machine currently strains.
4. Pure infrastructure: no joint-vs-marginal or null-hypothesis content; no applied domain.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Bubenik–Scott categorification (1205.3669) is the direct ancestor; Rota's Galois connection theory; multiparameter persistence literature. No cross-domain engagement — foundational TDA only.
