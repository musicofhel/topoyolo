## 2201.09385 --- Lim & Mémoli (2022)
**"Classical Multidimensional Scaling on Metric Measure Spaces"**
arXiv: 2201.09385 | math.FA

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: cMDS is itself a distance-structure-preserving embedding problem — the paper generalizes it to arbitrary metric measure spaces, placing it in the same relational-setting family as GW (the stability is even measured *in* GW distance). The spectral theory of the cMDS operator is the linear-algebraic sibling of the quadratic-matching machinery.

- **Stability**: The main theorem: the generalized cMDS process is stable with respect to the Gromov-Wasserstein distance — small relational perturbation of the input mm-space yields small output change of the embedding. Perturbation robustness proven *between* two instantiations of the matching family (cMDS output controlled by GW on inputs).

- **Null hypothesis** (weak): the sum of negative eigenvalues of the cMDS operator is a new invariant measuring non-flatness — a scalar statistic that is exactly zero precisely when the mm-space embeds isometrically in Euclidean space, i.e. a test quantity whose vanishing characterizes the "no residual structure" reference case.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Spectral invariant (negative eigenvalue sum) as non-flatness measure: a computable scalar summary of how far relational structure fails to flatten, with exact characterization on spheres S^{d-1} and Euclidean subsets.
2. Stability proven across machines (GW on inputs → cMDS outputs) rather than within one construction — the kind of cross-instantiation control the atlas's Matching≠Stability ANTISYNONYMS entry asks for as evidence.
3. No chain complex, parameterized homology, or joint-vs-marginal content.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Classical Torgerson/Gower cMDS lineage plus Sturm's GW framework; notes the shape/image-analysis use of the Euclidean embedding. No cross-domain engagement.
