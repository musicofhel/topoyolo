## liu-2025 — Liu, Xu, Li, Si, Yang, Yang, Zhao (2025)
**"Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Time Series Granger Causality Inference (KANGCI)"**

**Domain(s)**: Neuroscience (fMRI/EEG applications), information theory (Granger causality)

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: Granger causality proper: time series j Granger-causes i when past values of j improve prediction of i beyond i's own marginal history — an explicitly conditional (excess-over-marginal) test. The KAN component-wise NAR model with group lasso makes this excess a per-edge learnable quantity; nonzero lagged weights across j's inputs are the detected joint structure.
- **Null hypothesis**: Two nulls. (1) Group sparsity penalty Θ(W) encodes "no causal link" as exactly-zero edge weights — the destroyed-structure reference is the pruned/sparse model. (2) The time-reversed Granger algorithm uses reversal as a falsification probe: true causal directions are asymmetric under time reversal, so agreement between forward and reversed inferences filters spurious connections.
- **Chain complex (weak)**: The inferred adjacency matrix over interacting series is a directed causal graph; KAN's learnable edge functions make the graph's edges themselves the learned object (unlike MLP nodes). No homology is computed on the graph.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- First extension of Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks to Granger causality: learnable univariate functions on edges + summation nodes give a smaller computational graph than MLP, aiding limited-sample inference.
- Automatic selection-or-fusion between original and time-reversed causal adjacency matrices to mitigate spurious edges — a practical asymmetry-based consistency filter rather than a theoretical result.
- Validated on Lorenz-96, gene regulatory networks, fMRI BOLD, VAR and EEG. Contribution is architectural; the machine structure it exploits (conditional-prediction excess, sparsity-as-null) is inherited from the cMLP/eSRU/GVAR lineage.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Cites Seth, Friston, Barnett & Seth (computational neuroscience GC tradition), Tank et al. cMLP, Khanna & Tan eSRU, Bussmann NAVAR. Fully within the neural-Granger silo; no TDA, QEC or topology connection acknowledged. Sibling of wang-2024 (GC-STCL) and GVAR/Marinazzo entries in composite_systems.
