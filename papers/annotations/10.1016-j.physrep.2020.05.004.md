## 10.1016/j.physrep.2020.05.004 --- Battiston, Cencetti, Iacopini, Latora, Lucas, Patania, Young, Petri (2020)
**"Networks beyond pairwise interactions: Structure and dynamics"**
DOI: 10.1016/j.physrep.2020.05.004 | Physics Reports 874:1–92

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Chain complex**: The survey's structural backbone is the simplicial-complex/hypergraph chain apparatus: incidence and adjacency matrices generalizing to higher-order tensors, simplicial homology on clique complexes, boundary operators acting on oriented simplices, and the higher-order Laplacians (combinatorial and normalized) that act as the Hodge-style operators of the k-form chain spaces. Section 3's measures are explicitly graded by simplex dimension — Betti numbers and spectral properties of higher-order Laplacians are read as invariants of the boundary-operator sequence.
- **Null hypothesis**: Generative models for higher-order structures — random bipartite graph projections, configuration-model hypergraphs, growing preferential-attachment simplicial complexes — serve throughout as the constrained-random ensembles against which empirical higher-order signatures (heavy-tailed simplex-size distributions, non-random cliquishness) are detected.
- **Parameterized homology (weak)**: The review repeatedly tracks topological invariants across a driving parameter — percolation and diffusion processes whose order parameter is simplex dimension or coupling strength, with abrupt (explosive, discontinuous) transitions in connectivity and contagion emerging only when interactions go beyond pairwise. The invariant (giant-component structure, epidemic threshold) changes character at critical parameter values.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The canonical unification of hypergraph vs simplicial-complex representations, with explicit maps between them (bipartite projections, clique complexes, line graphs).
- The systematic catalogue of qualitatively new phenomena from higher-order interactions: explosive (discontinuous) percolation transitions, abrupt contagion onset, synchronization patterns inaccessible to dyadic networks (conensus/antisynchronization states), higher-order fixed points in evolutionary games.
- Higher-order centrality and distance measures defined directly on the boundary-operator/Laplacian machinery rather than by projection to graphs.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Simplicial complexes in network theory (Petri, Barrat, Bianconi), topological signals and higher-order Laplacian spectra (Schaub, Benson), synchronization theory, social-network sociology (Simmel). No connections to QEC; neuroscience appears via brain-connectivity applications.
