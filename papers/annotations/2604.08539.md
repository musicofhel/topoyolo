## 2604.08539 --- Hu et al. (2026)
**"OpenVLThinkerV2: A Generalist Multimodal Reasoning Model for Multi-domain Visual Tasks"**
arXiv: 2604.08539

**Domain(s)**: Machine learning, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: Gaussian GRPO (G²RPO) is explicitly a 1D optimal transport construction: instead of linearly standardizing the advantage distribution (moment matching only), it solves for a non-linear map that forces each task's advantage distribution onto a fixed target measure, the standard normal N(0,1). This is an optimal assignment/coupling between the empirical reward-derived distribution on the real line and a reference distribution, applied per task so that gradient updates are equalized across tasks with different reward topologies (sparse binary vs dense IoU).
- **Stability**: The paper's central stability claim is robustness of the training update under perturbation of the reward distribution: heavy-tailed outlier advantages, which under GRPO produce disproportionate (catastrophic) parameter updates, are bounded once the distributional matching to N(0,1) is enforced, because the coupling compresses tail mass symmetrically. Entropy shaping similarly bounds the exploration zone against both entropy collapse and entropy explosion — two-sided perturbation control on a policy-intrinsic quantity.
- **Null hypothesis**: Weaker reading, noted for completeness: N(0,1) functions as a structure-free reference distribution that each task's advantage distribution is re-expressed against, stripping task-specific reward topology before updates are compared across tasks. It is constructed by transformation rather than by destroying structure in data, so this is a marginal instantiation at best.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The observation that linear rescaling (mean/variance standardization, EMA-based variance estimates) preserves higher-order distributional shape and therefore cannot equalize gradient flow across tasks — the fix must be a full distributional coupling, not moment matching. This gap between moment-matching and measure-matching is stated as an RL engineering problem, but its content is exactly the W_1-vs-shape distinction the Rosetta's optimal-transport cell tracks (cf. QR-DQN's quantile/Wasserstein line).
- Task-level response-length shaping as a perception/reasoning balancing mechanism has no analogue among the six machines; it is a curriculum-on-output-form heuristic.
- Everything else (model architecture, benchmark results) reduces to standard MLLM post-training.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: GRPO/DR.GRPO/EMA-GRPO lineage, distributional RL implicitly via reward-distribution language. No citation of optimal transport literature, TDA, or any cross-domain parallel — the 1D OT appears unnamed (described only as "non-linear distributional matching").
