## 1112.4589 --- Qian & Roy (2011)
**"An Information Theoretical Analysis of Kinase Activated Phosphorylation Dephosphorylation Cycle"**
arXiv: 1112.4589 | q-bio.SC, cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: Information theory, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: Channel capacity computation for the PdPC module is an optimization of input-output coupling between kinase drive and substrate-state distribution; the multistage-code reading of a linear signaling cascade treats each phosphorylation step as a coding stage whose concatenated channel the cascade must match signal through. Trading channel capacity against code complexity with cascade depth is a resource-constrained assignment problem across stages.

- **Parameterized homology**: The headline result — channel capacity is zero iff free-energy expenditure is zero, with positive correlation in between — makes capacity a function tracked along the dissipation parameter. The capacity-vs-expenditure curve is a computable one-parameter family whose zero point is exactly thermodynamic equilibrium; the "curve crossing zero at a physically meaningful parameter value" is the machine's signature.

- **Null hypothesis**: Zero free-energy expenditure is the destroyed-structure reference: at equilibrium detailed balance erases the driven non-equilibrium structure, and the theorem states communication capacity vanishes exactly there. Equilibrium is the null channel; driving it breaks the null and buys capacity.

- **Joint-vs-marginal excess** (instrumental): The distributed multistage code claim — a cascade of modules carries information no single stage carries alone, with energy spent at every stage to maintain the joint channel — frames cellular signaling as composite-system structure absent from individual reaction marginals.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. The capacity↔dissipation equivalence: a Shannon capacity is zero if and only if the underlying biochemical system burns no free energy — arguably the cleanest single statement connecting information theory and nonequilibrium thermodynamics in the corpus.
2. Cascades as distributed multistage codes: an operational, information-theoretic reason why biology pays for multi-step phosphorylation cascades (complexity bought at capacity cost), not just amplification folklore.
3. The dictum that static codes can live in molecular structure but biochemical channels must be energetically maintained — information as a dissipative resource.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Nonequilibrium statistical mechanics of driven biochemical cycles (the authors' own stochastic-dynamics program), Shannon's channel theory, Hopfield/Ninio kinetic-proofreading-style energy-for-accuracy arguments. Explicit InfoTheo↔StatPhys bridge landing in biological territory; no TDA/OT/QEC content.
