## 2205.08782 --- Bereyhi, Loureiro, Krzakala, Müller & Schulz-Baldes (2022)
**"Secure Coding via Gaussian Random Fields"**
arXiv: 2205.08782 | cs.IT

**Domain(s)**: Information theory, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: The replica analysis locates a critical rate at which Bayesian inference over nonlinear Gaussian random fields exhibits an all-or-nothing transition — perfect recovery below, total decorrelation above. The critical rate is then identified with the channel capacity: an invariant (capacity) read off as the crossing point of a parameterized family of inference curves.

- **Null hypothesis**: The above-critical regime is the destroyed-structure reference: past the critical rate the optimal estimator's output becomes uncorrelated with the data — all signal destroyed while the observation model is otherwise unchanged. Capacity is exactly where this null sets in.

- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The wiretap application makes secrecy a joint-vs-marginal statement: random binning encodes so that the legitimate receiver's joint distribution carries the message while the eavesdropper's marginal is (asymptotically) independent of it — secrecy capacity is the rate at which the excess mutual information between joint and leaked marginal vanishes.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. The exact identification: the all-or-nothing critical rate of a spin-glass-style inference problem IS the channel capacity — not analogy but equality via the replica computation.
2. A constructive use of the correspondence: the same Gaussian-random-field model plus random binning yields a secure coding scheme asymptotically achieving the Gaussian wiretap secrecy capacity.
3. Large-system (replica) characterization placing wiretap security inside the phase-transition picture rather than beside it.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Replica method from spin-glass theory; all-or-nothing literature on nonlinear random-field inverse problems; wiretap channel secrecy-capacity formalism. No TDA/OT content.
