## 2210.11547 --- Kelly, Poschinger, Schmidt-Kaler, Fisher & Marino (2022)
**"Coherence requirements for quantum communication from hybrid circuit dynamics"**
arXiv: 2210.11547 | quant-ph/cond-mat

**Domain(s)**: Quantum, information theory, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: Entanglement and quantum channel capacity are tracked as functions of the coherence resource budget granted to each player in a hybrid unitary-vs-measurement circuit; the discovery is a coherence-tuned phase transition — the capacity invariant jumps at a critical coherence level rather than degrading smoothly.

- **Null hypothesis**: Eve's measurement-limited strategy is the destroyed-structure reference: measurements applied so as to destroy channel capacity while leaving the qubit count and dynamics otherwise fixed. Alice's requirements are defined relative to how much structure survives this adversarial null.

- **Stability**: The main theorem gives a minimum-coherence bound for any successful Alice strategy, and the corollary bounds code distance in stabilizer QEC codes by available coherence — a rigorous robustness criterion: how much encoded-information protection (code distance) a resource constraint permits.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. The adversarial-game formulation: monitored dynamics AND error correction as two-player unitary-vs-measurement competition over channel capacity, unifying both settings in one circuit model.
2. A coherence-tuned phase transition in entanglement/capacity — coherence itself, not measurement rate, as the order parameter.
3. The theorem chain: minimum coherence for successful communication → coherence upper-bounds stabilizer code distance — a resource-theoretic cap on QEC protection.
