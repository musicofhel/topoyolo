## 2401.00885 --- Hart (2023)
**"Attractor reconstruction with reservoir computers: The effect of the reservoir's conditional Lyapunov exponents on faithful attractor reconstruction"**
arXiv: 2401.00885 | cs.LG/nlin.CD

**Domain(s)**: dynamical systems

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The paper's whole content is a quantitative stability condition on the reconstruction correspondence: faithful attractor reconstruction and Lyapunov-spectrum estimation require the driven reservoir's largest conditional Lyapunov exponent to be significantly more negative than the most negative Lyapunov exponent of the target system. The quality of the matching between reservoir attractor and source attractor is controlled by an explicit spectral gap in contraction rates — a perturbation-scale inequality, not just a qualitative "it works".
- **Matching** (weak): Reconstruction itself is the generalized-synchronization correspondence of `annotations/2108.05024.md`; this paper characterizes *when* that correspondence is faithful, i.e. the error side of the assignment rather than its existence. (Depth-limited: abstract gives the inequality empirically on Lorenz-type systems; no theorem visible at abstract level.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- A tunable architectural parameter (spectral radius of the reservoir adjacency matrix) is tied to a dynamical invariant (maximal conditional Lyapunov exponent) and thence to reconstruction fidelity — a three-term chain from hyperparameter to stability guarantee that none of the six machines encodes by themselves.
- The practical corollary — small-spectral-radius reservoirs reconstruct better in general — inverts the folk wisdom that edge-of-chaos spectral radii are optimal, showing the optimality criterion is task-dependent (reconstruction vs. computation).

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Reservoir computing / echo-state networks; generalized synchronization theory (including `annotations/2108.05024.md`, same group); Lyapunov-spectrum estimation from reconstructed attractors. No acknowledgment of TDA or OT literatures.
