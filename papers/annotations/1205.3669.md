## 1205.3669 --- Bubenik & Scott (2012)
**"Categorification of persistent homology"**
arXiv: 1205.3669 | math.AT

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: The core object is precisely the Rosetta machine — persistence modules as diagrams indexed by the poset (ℝ, ≤) in an arbitrary target category. PH stops being a construction on simplicial complexes and becomes the study of such diagrams; extended persistence and kernel/image/cokernel persistence are all instances of the same diagram formalism.

- **Stability**: The interleaving distance on diagrams is shown to generalize the bottleneck distance, and the stability theorem is thereby *greatly generalized*: any target category with an interleaving notion inherits stability for free. Stability ceases to be a per-construction inequality and becomes a categorical property of the indexing.

- **Matching**: The bottleneck matching underlying the classical distance is subsumed: interleavings are the categorified matchings, and the paper builds a category *of* interleavings (abelian whenever the target is), giving matchings themselves compositional structure.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Category-of-interleavings: not just a distance but an abelian category structure on the perturbation relation itself — kernels/cokernels of perturbations exist, so stability proofs become homological algebra.
2. Domain-independence of persistence: because diagrams may land in ANY category, persistence machinery detaches from topology entirely (the paper notes this enables applications well outside PH).
3. Nothing here instantiates joint-vs-marginal excess or null hypothesis; it is pure infrastructure for two machines (PH + Stability) plus the matching substrate.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Explicitly generalizes bottleneck stability (Cohen-Steiner–Edelsbrunner–Harer), extended persistence, kernel/image/cokernel persistence (Carlsson–de Silva). No cross-domain engagement — but as the categorical backbone it is upstream of every later applied-PH entry in this atlas.
