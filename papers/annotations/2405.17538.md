## 2405.17538 --- Howard, Klinger, Maiti & Stapleton (2024)
**"Bayesian RG Flow in Neural Network Field Theories"**
arXiv: 2405.17538 | hep-th, cs.LG

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics, information theory, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Parameterized homology**: Coarse-graining is performed in *parameter space* against a distinguishability scale set by the Fisher information metric — an explicit filtration parameter on the space of distributions. Information shells play the role of momentum shells: sweeping the cutoff sweeps a scale-parameterized family of effective theories, and training itself induces a flow (IR→UV) that is the same object read in the opposite direction.
- **Stability**: The BRG's equivalence to exact RG exactly when the information-theoretic cutoff coincides with a momentum scale is a persistence statement — which coarse-grained descriptions survive a change of cutoff scheme is precisely the stability-of-invariants-under-reparameterization question.
- **Matching**: The NNFT correspondence itself is a term-matched map between two spaces (NN architectures ↔ statistical field theories) under which flows correspond to flows; the paper's content is that this correspondence can be made structure-preserving rather than metaphorical.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The Fisher metric as the *native* distinguishability scale for coarse-graining arbitrary parameterized distributions — generalizing ERG beyond field-theory parametrizations without appealing to physical length at all.
- The bidirectional reading: training = IR→UV, coarse-graining = UV→IR. Prior rg-dl work (1410.3831) maps architecture onto RG; this maps *dynamics* onto the flow, in both directions.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: NNFT correspondence, Bayesian renormalization group, Wilsonian exact RG / momentum-shell integration, infinite-width NTK regimes. Written from hep-th into ML theory; no TDA/QEC/OT awareness. Part of the rg-dl lineage descending from 1410.3831 but replaces the RBM-stack identity with an information-geometric one.
