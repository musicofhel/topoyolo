## 2110.08631 --- Smith, Kim, Lu, Bassett (2021)
**"Learning Continuous Chaotic Attractors with a Reservoir Computer"**
arXiv: 2110.08631 | cs.NE

**Domain(s)**: dynamical systems

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: A reservoir computer trained on isolated examples of an attractor (stable limit cycles; chaotic Lorenz systems at different parameter shifts) learns not a point in hypothesis space but a *continuum* — a one-parameter family of attractors indexed by the training shift. The learned correspondence maps a continuous label/shift axis to attractor states, i.e. generalized synchronization whose image is a differentiably-parametrized family rather than a single conjugate copy. The matching is between a continuous input manifold and the reservoir's attractor manifold.
- **Stability**: The signature of successful abstraction is dynamical: an extra Lyapunov exponent equal to zero. Neutral stability along the family direction is exactly what distinguishes "a continuum of attractors" from "one memorized attractor plus noise" — the stability spectrum is the certificate that the correspondence is genuinely parametrized.
- **Null hypothesis**: The zero exponent is also a falsifiable criterion — an RC that merely memorized isolated training attractors would show only negative exponents; the null (memorization) is rejected by reading the spectrum.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Abstraction operationalized as a spectral quantity: "the system abstracted" is made equivalent to "a zero Lyapunov exponent appeared," turning a cognitive-science notion into a measurable invariant of a trained dynamical system.
- Mechanism claimed via *differentiable* generalized synchronization + feedback dynamics: the family direction arises because GS is smooth in the drive parameter, so the reservoir inherits parametrized families from parametrized training data. This extends `annotations/2108.05024.md` (embedding IS synchronization) from single-attractor embedding to families-of-attractor abstraction.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: reservoir computing (Jaeger lineage), generalized synchronization theory, Lyapunov analysis, neural basis of abstraction / mental manipulation literature. No TDA, QEC, or OT.
