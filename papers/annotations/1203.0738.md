## 1203.0738 --- Dehghani, Hatsopoulos, Haga, Parker, Greger, Halgren, Cash & Destexhe (2012)
**"Avalanche analysis from multi-electrode ensemble recordings in cat, monkey and human cerebral cortex during wakefulness and sleep"**
arXiv: 1203.0738 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Null hypothesis**: The empirical half of the structured disagreement: applying the rigorous (CDF-based, alternative-distribution) test discipline to in vivo data, unit-defined avalanches never scale as power law — exponential or intermediate scaling instead — and nLFP apparent power laws fail closer examination, with pLFP peaks (less tied to firing) showing the same apparent scaling; bi-exponential fits win throughout. The null here is the fitted-alternative distribution family: "the tail is bi-exponential," against which the power-law reading is rejected across preparations.
- **Stability**: A negative-stability result: the power-law verdict does not persist across measurement channel (units vs nLFP vs pLFP), species, or brain state (wake/slow-wave/REM). The one quantity that IS stable is the failure of power-law scaling — invariance of the rejection under every perturbation tested.
- **Matching**: Implicitly, the distribution-model selection problem: candidate families (power law, exponential, bi-exponential) matched to empirical CDFs by rigorous statistical measures rather than by regression on log axes.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Scale and generality of the refutation: high-density arrays across three species, wakefulness and sleep — the methodological null of 0910.0805 carried from analysis critique to empirical verdict on the actual claimed domain (awake mammalian cortex).
- The measurement-channel control: pLFP peaks reproduce the nLFP apparent power laws despite being less directly related to neuronal firing — evidence that the scaling shape tracks the thresholding operation, not the underlying collective dynamics (the 0910.0805 mechanism confirmed in vivo).
- Bi-exponential as the winning alternative: a concrete, named non-power-law description of avalanche statistics that any future criticality claim must beat.
