## 1410.3831 --- Mehta & Schwab (2014)
**"An exact mapping between the Variational Renormalization Group and Deep Learning"**
arXiv: 1410.3831 | stat.ML, cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Parameterized homology**: The variational RG step IS a scale-parameterized object: each Kadanoff coarse-graining stage acts on field configurations exactly as one layer of an RBM stack acts on its hidden representations. Layer index ↔ length scale gives deep learning a built-in scale parameter, with "relevant features" = structures persisting as the parameter advances.
- **Stability**: The relevant-operator/irrelevant-operator split the RG performs is stability under iterated coarse-graining — operators are classified by whether their couplings grow or shrink along the flow — so feature learning inherits a persistence-under-the-flow criterion rather than an accuracy metric.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The EXACT correspondence: not an analogy but a term-by-term identity between the variational RG equations and the RBM stack's greedy layer-wise training (weights ↔ couplings, hidden units ↔ coarse-grained degrees of freedom).
- The claim that deep learning "may be employing a generalized RG-like scheme" — the founding hypothesis statement for the whole rg-dl corpus lineage.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Kadanoff's variational RG, RBMs and Hinton-style greedy pre-training, Ising model.
