## 1305.1742 --- Witthaut & Timme (2013)
**"Kuramoto dynamics in Hamiltonian systems"**
arXiv: 1305.1742 | nlin.CD

**Domain(s)**: dynamical systems; statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The synchronization transition is re-derived as a *transverse instability* of Hamiltonian action dynamics restricted to an invariant manifold — the same critical coupling, obtained from stability of an embedded conservative system instead of dissipative mean-field analysis. This is a change of machine for computing the stability boundary, not merely a new example.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The 2N-variable Hamiltonian system carries N-dimensional invariant manifolds whose induced (marginal) dynamics is exactly Kuramoto; everything transverse to the manifold is composite-level structure with no analogue in the marginal description, and it is precisely that excess which decides whether synchrony sets in.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- An exact (not approximate) embedding of dissipative Kuramoto dynamics inside a classical conservative system — a constructive bridge between the dissipative and conservative universes, with the sync transition located by Hamiltonian instability analysis.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Kuramoto literature, Hamiltonian/action-angle methods, power-grid oscillator applications (Timme group lineage). No TDA, QEC, OT, or ML.
