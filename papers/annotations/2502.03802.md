## 2502.03802 --- Zhang, Mirallès, Rousseau-Rizzi, Zinflou, Wu & Boulet (2025)
**"MXMap: A Multivariate Cross Mapping Framework for Causal Discovery in Dynamical Systems"**
arXiv: 2502.03802 | cs.LG, math.DS

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: The core machinery stays CCM's nearest-neighbor cross mapping between Takens-reconstructed state spaces; multiPCM's move is to *multivariate* delay embeddings — the library vectors live in a higher-dimensional reconstructed space built from several observed series at once, so the matching is between richer shadows of the joint dynamics rather than per-variable shadows.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: Direct-vs-indirect causality is operationalized as a conditional comparison — does the cross-mapping quality between X and Y survive conditioning on intermediate variables Z? The causal signal is precisely the excess structure in the joint (X,Y | Z) embedding that the marginal (X,Y) pairing overstates as direct.
- **Null hypothesis**: The two-phase design runs an explicit negative control: pairwise CCM establishes an initial graph whose edges are presumed indirect until multiPCM prunes them — the pruning step tests each edge against the null "this link is fully mediated," and the framework's claimed advantage is a lower false-indirect-link rate.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Multivariate embedding inside partial cross mapping: PCM itself was the corpus-known refinement (conditioning to kill indirect links), but it was confined to univariate embeddings; lifting the conditioning test into multivariate libraries is a representational upgrade no other CCM-variant entry makes.
- Two-phase graph workflow (over-generate then prune) rather than a single test statistic: causality discovery becomes graph-refinement, which changes what "the null" means per edge.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: CCM (Sugihara et al.) and PCM as direct ancestors; compared against Granger-class and constraint-based baselines on ERA5 reanalysis data. No Rosetta-cross-domain awareness.
