## 2103.00503 --- Nakazato, Ito (2021)
**"Geometrical aspects of entropy production in stochastic thermodynamics based on Wasserstein distance"**
arXiv: 2103.00503 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: Statistical physics, optimal transport

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching** (instrumental): The L2-Wasserstein distance is the geometric currency of the whole result — defined, as ever, by an optimal coupling between the initial and final measures on the driving path. But the coupling itself is not the object of study; it is consumed inside a lower bound. What the paper contributes is the *identification*: entropy production ≥ action measured by the Wasserstein path length of the density trajectory. Dissipation cost = length of the cheapest matching path — the Matching machine appears as the geometry in which a thermodynamic quantity becomes readable.
- **Joint-vs-marginal** (weak): The subsystem formalism derives a lower bound on *partial* entropy production, generalizing the second law of information thermodynamics — dissipation attributable to a component of a composite can exceed what its marginal dynamics would suggest. (Depth-limited at abstract level; the full joint/marginal additivity story is candidate-07 Ito–Oizumi–Amari's explicit subject.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The trade-off structure: transition time × entropy production bound gives a thermodynamic speed limit tighter than prior bounds. Speed limits are a *constraint family*, not any single machine — they say how fast a matching between measures can be physically driven as a function of its dissipation, which couples Matching to real-time dynamics in a way none of the six machines encode.
- The optimal-protocol geometry: minimizing entropy production becomes a geometric problem (shortest admissible path in Wasserstein space), with the optimal stochastic heat engine as the worked instance and a geometrical efficiency bound. The atlas has no machine for "physical cost of driving a coupling."
- Bridge value: this is the cleanest single equation linking the Matching machine to a non-epistemic, physical quantity. In TDA/QEC/information-theory instantiations the matching cost measures inference or correction effort; here the same functional measures irreversibility — heat, fuel, dissipation. That is a semantic divergence worth tracking (see ANTI-candidate note below).

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Optimal transport theory (Benamou–Brenier dynamic formulation implicitly, via the action/path-length form); stochastic thermodynamics (Seifert lineage); information thermodynamics (partial entropy production, Sagawa–Ueda line). No acknowledgment of TDA, QEC, or persistence.
