## 1004.2515 --- Williams & Beer (2010)
**"Nonnegative Decomposition of Multivariate Information"**
arXiv: 1004.2515 | cs.IT

**Domain(s)**: information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Chain complex**: THE founding instance of the redundancy-lattice algebra the whole corpus inherits: sources are organized into an antichain poset ordered by subset-superset relations, and partial information atoms are recovered from cumulative I_min values by Möbius inversion over that lattice — exactly the graded-poset-plus-inversion skeleton Rosetta has been calling chain-complex-like since the PID prose blocks.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The paper's motivating pathology IS the machine: McGill's interaction information can go negative because it conflates redundancy and synergy — i.e. it cannot tell joint-level surplus from component-level overlap. The redundancy lattice is constructed precisely to separate those two directions of joint-vs-marginal structure, and synergy emerges as the top-of-lattice atom absent marginally.
- **Matching**: I_min defines redundancy as the *minimum* per-outcome information any single source provides — a worst-case aggregation over sources rather than an average, foreshadowing the min-coupling flavour of later matching-side operationalizations (BROJA et al. optimize against such a min).

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The redundancy lattice itself and the non-negativity theorem for its atoms — an axiomatic object with no analogue in the other five domains; every annotated PID paper consumes this lattice as given.
- The diagnosis of interaction information's negativity as redundancy/synergy confounding — the first explicit statement that naive joint-minus-marginal bookkeeping mixes two distinct structures.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Interaction information (McGill 1954), multivariate generalizations of Shannon information. Written entirely inside information theory — no TDA, QEC, dynamical systems, or ML awareness. Abstract-only provenance — depth-limited.
