## 2511.11092 --- Seely (2025)
**"Sheaf Cohomology of Linear Predictive Coding Networks"**
arXiv: 2511.11092 | cs.LG

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience, TDA, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Chain complex**: Linear predictive coding IS a cellular sheaf: the coboundary operator maps node activations to edge-wise prediction errors; PC inference is exactly diffusion under the sheaf Laplacian (δᵀδ). The paper's central object is the resulting cochain complex and its cohomology.
- **Null hypothesis**: Cohomology classes are characterized as *irreducible* error patterns — error that no amount of inference (diffusion) can remove. This is a null-by-construction reading: H¹ is precisely what survives after the learnable/removable part of the error has been exhausted by the Laplacian flow.
- **Stability**: The Hodge decomposition splits the error space into gradient / co-gradient / harmonic components; learning stalls are located in the harmonic component, giving a diagnostic for which network topologies (feedback loops) make failure structural rather than incidental.
- **Parameterized homology**: Recurrent topologies are analyzed as configurations whose cohomology changes as feedback edges are added/removed — topology-of-the-complex determines which contradictions can arise.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The first corpus entry where cohomology itself — not just the Laplacian or persistence — carries the semantic load: "error inference cannot remove" is an invariant of the complex, not an optimization artifact.
- A genuine Rosetta bridge: local-learning neuroscience (predictive coding) re-derived as sheaf diffusion on a chain complex, with the Hodge decomposition repurposed as a learning-dynamics diagnostic (when do internal contradictions stall learning?). Neither community's vocabulary appears in the other.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Cites cellular sheaf theory and predictive-coding literature (Rao–Ballard lineage implicitly); the sheaf-Laplacian-as-GNN line from Hansen–Gebhart (2012.06333). No TDA/persistence awareness.
