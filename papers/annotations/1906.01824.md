## 1906.01824 --- Mukherjee, Asnani, Kannan (2019)
**"CCMI: Classifier Based Conditional Mutual Information Estimation"**
arXiv: 1906.01824 | UAI 2019

**Domain(s)**: Information theory, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The paper's object is the conditional version of the joint-vs-marginal gap. CMI is expressed as a KL divergence I(X;Y|Z) = D_KL(p_xyz || q_xyz) between the true conditional joint p_xyz = p(z)p(x|z)p(y|x,z) and the "conditional product" null q_xyz = p(z)p(x|z)p(y|z) — the distribution obtained by severing the X–Y link while preserving both conditionals on Z. The estimator trains a classifier to distinguish samples from these two distributions; the logit of the trained classifier is the estimated density ratio, and the divergence is the measured excess.
- **Null hypothesis**: Conditional independence is the explicit null: X ⊥ Y | Z ⟺ I(X;Y|Z) = 0. The paper operationalizes this into a conditional-independence tester whose performance is benchmarked against the state of the art, with the conditional product distribution serving as the sampled null. The Markov-chain examples (X→Z→Y gives independence; X→Z←Y does not) are precisely the discriminations the null must encode.
- **Stability (weak)**: The empirical claim is dimensional robustness — estimates remain accurate up to 100 dimensions where KSG degrades beyond 5 — i.e., the estimator's variance is controlled under growth of the ambient dimension, a perturbation-robustness property of the measurement device rather than of an annotated structure.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Lifting the MINE-style classifier-dual estimation from MI to CMI via conditional generative models (CGANs, CVAEs) as candidate samplers for the conditional-product null — the sampling of the null is itself learned.
- Divergence-based vs difference-based CMI estimators, with the difference construction (I(X;YZ) − I(X;Z)) empirically strongest.
- Dimensional scaling result: accuracy held at 100d versus kNN/kernel collapse.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Cite MINE (Belghazi et al. 2018), KSG estimator, conditional generative models. Applications named in causal discovery, feature selection, graphical models. No connections to TDA, QEC, or dynamical systems.
