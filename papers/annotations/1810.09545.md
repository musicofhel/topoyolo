## 1810.09545 --- Ito, Oizumi, Amari (2018)
**"Unified framework for the entropy production and the stochastic interaction based on information geometry"**
arXiv: 1810.09545 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: Statistical physics, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The paper's central identity is exactly this machine in thermodynamic clothing: *violation of additivity* of the subsystem partial entropy productions — total entropy production ≠ sum of the parts — is shown to be the stochastic interaction of integrated information theory. Structure present in the composite system's dissipation but absent from its components is not a diagnostic curiosity here; it is given a physical reading (irreversible cost of statistical dependence between subsystems). The rare instance where the joint-vs-marginal gap carries units of heat rather than bits.
- **Matching** (weak): The information-geometric formalism yields a novel expression of entropy production as an optimization problem minimizing a Kullback–Leibler divergence — a projection/assignment onto the closest structured (product-form or Markov) reference within an information-geometric manifold. (Depth-limited: abstract-level; spin-model illustration only.)
- **Null hypothesis** (implicit): The additive decomposition plays the reference role — the "destroyed structure" baseline against which the composite's excess dissipation is measured.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- A thermodynamic *foundation* for integrated information theory: stochastic interaction, previously an information measure, is identified with non-additivity of entropy production. This grounds a contested informational quantity in fluctuation-theorem physics — a bridge Neuroscience↔Statistical-physics that runs through neither TDA nor coding theory.
- The dual-currency claim itself: the same geometric object (information-geometric divergence between true dynamics and its disintegrated reference) measures both informational integration and physical irreversibility. None of the six machines encodes *which substrate* the excess lives on; this paper shows the choice of substrate can be swapped while the excess persists.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Integrated information theory (Oizumi lineage, explicitly); information geometry (Amari, explicitly — a rare authorial cross-domain bridge); stochastic thermodynamics (entropy production formalism). This is one of the few corpus papers whose authors deliberately span communities.
