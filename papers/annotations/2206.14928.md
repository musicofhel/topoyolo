## 2206.14928 --- Huguet, Magruder, Tong, Fasina, Kuchroo, Wolf, Krishnaswamy (2022)
**"Manifold Interpolating Optimal-Transport Flows for Trajectory Inference"**
arXiv: 2206.14928 | cs.LG

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems, optimal transport

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: The training signal is a dynamic-optimal-transport coupling between population snapshots sampled at sporadic timepoints: a Neural ODE generates continuous trajectories whose induced transport is penalized against OT computed with a *manifold ground distance* (a novel multiscale geodesic distance on the data manifold) rather than Euclidean cost. The assignment being solved is measure-to-measure between empirical populations, with the cost matrix itself carrying the latent-geometry information — the same degeneracy-shrinking move as 1907.08260 (side information beyond marginals), here injected through the ground cost instead of through delay-coordinate side channels.
- **Stability** (weak): Operating in the latent space of the geodesic autoencoder is explicitly a robustness device — interpolating in a space where distances track the data manifold keeps the flow on the support of observed populations rather than drifting through unobserved regions; evaluation on simulated data with bifurcations and merges tests that inferred trajectories survive genuine topology change. (Depth-limited: abstract-level, no perturbation bound visible.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The geodesic autoencoder: regularizing *latent* pairwise distances to match a multiscale geodesic distance on the data manifold. This makes the OT ground cost and the representation learning a single coupled object — neither machine describes that coupling; Matching presupposes a fixed cost, Stability presupposes a metric under which perturbations are measured. Here both are learned jointly.
- The noise-to-data vs population-to-population distinction the authors themselves draw: normalizing flows and Schrödinger bridges flow from a prior to data, whereas trajectory inference needs couplings between two data-supported measures. That reframing — which endpoint distribution you are allowed to fix — is an orthogonal modeling commitment not encoded by the machines.
- Where it sits relative to batch-004's bridge pair: 2409.08768 pulls dynamics into OT language (pushforward of measures); MIOFlow pushes OT back into dynamics (a continuous-time flow whose path realizes the dynamic-OT coupling). With 03 TrajectoryNet rejected as the thinner wrapper, this closes the Dynamics×Matching group with the strongest instance of the applied direction.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Dynamic optimal transport theory (Benamou–Brenier lineage, stated as the theoretical link); manifold learning / diffusion geometry (the multiscale geodesic distance descends from diffusion maps tradition — Krishnaswamy lab lineage); neural ODEs; comparison class includes Schrödinger bridges. Applications to scRNA-seq (embryoid body, AML treatment). No acknowledgment of TDA or QEC.
