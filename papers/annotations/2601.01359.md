## 2601.01359 --- Kawamura, Majhi, Mitra (2026)
**"The Shadow of Vietoris–Rips Complexes in Limits"**
arXiv: 2601.01359 | math.AT

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Chain complex**: simplicial homology of the shadow S(R_β(X)) — the union of convex hulls of simplices of the Vietoris–Rips complex with vertices in R^N — and of the abstract complexes themselves; homology groups are the invariants the limit theorems transport.

- **Parameterized homology**: the scale β is exactly the filtration parameter, but the paper's move is to treat the *system* {S(R_β(S)) | β>0, S⊂X} as an inverse system (shape theory) and take limits in β→0 *jointly* with sample densification S→X — a two-parameter refinement of the usual one-parameter barcode picture.

- **Stability**: reconstruction robustness lineage — Hausmann (M ≃ R_β(M) for small β), Latschev (d_GH(S,M)<δ(β) ⟹ R_β(S)≃M), and quantitative curvature-bounded extensions cited as the qualitative backdrop; the paper adds: even when each individual projection p: R_β(X) → S(R_β(X)) has unresolved singularities (resolved only for N≤3), the limit map lim p induces isomorphisms on homotopy/homology for X an ANR with regularity conditions. Stability recovered at the level of the limiting system rather than each finite stage.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. The observation that the combinatorial-to-geometric projection p itself carries "singularities" that obstruct naive transport of topological information from Rips complexes to their geometric shadows — a failure mode invisible if one only computes barcodes.
2. Shape-theoretic rescue: singularities of individual stages dissolve under inverse-limit machinery; well-behavedness is a property of the system, not its members. This is a stability mechanism distinct from bottleneck-distance perturbation bounds.
3. Implications stated for finite reconstruction of 1-dimensional submanifolds (metric graphs / Euclidean graphs).

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Hausmann, Latschev, Adamaszek–Adams Rips-programme line; Čech/α-complex comparisons; explicit motivation from TDA practice. No cross-domain (QEC/neuro/info-theory) awareness.
