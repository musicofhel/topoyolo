## 2203.10810 --- Wollstadt, Rathbun, Usrey, Bastos, Lindner, Priesemann, Wibral (2022)
**"Information-theoretic analyses of neural data to minimize the effect of researchers' assumptions in predictive coding studies"**
arXiv: 2203.10810 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: partial information decomposition applied to the transferred information — separating bottom-up sensory input from information transferred conditionally on the current state of the synapse (unique-information atom vs shared component), estimated directly from retinogeniculate spiking data rather than assumed from a model class.

- **Null hypothesis**: the circularity-breaking move itself — instead of testing "is predictive coding implemented?" while presupposing what is predicted, the paper states competing processing strategies as testable relationships between local active information storage and local transfer entropy, so the strategy hypothesis becomes a falsifiable constraint on measurable local information dynamics rather than an unfalsifiable correlate hunt.

- **Matching** (weak): the strategy-discrimination step is a model selection between two hypothesized couplings (predictable-input coding vs surprise coding) resolved by which coupling the measured AIS/TE profile matches.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. A methodological inversion: information-theoretic quantities are used not as features but as *assumption minimizers* — expressing a cognitive theory (predictive coding) in local information dynamics so that the theory's commitments become estimable from data, explicitly targeting researcher-assumption contamination.
2. The AIS-vs-TE relationship as a discriminant between opposing strategies — a two-hypothesis testable geometry on local information dynamics, demonstrated on cat retinogeniculate spiking (synapse codes for predictable rather than surprising input; preferentially bottom-up transfer).
3. No chain complex, parameterized homology, or matching-optimization content; PID enters as analysis tool, not as a new structural claim.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: explicit lineage within the Wibral/Lizier PID + information-dynamics programme (cf. 10.3390/e19090494, PID-05 2021 extension to continuous variables); engagement with the predictive-coding literature it critiques. No TDA/matching-domain awareness.
