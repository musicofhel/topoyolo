## 2407.02416 --- Buendía (2024)
**"A mesoscopic theory for stochastic coupled oscillators"**
arXiv: 2407.02416 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The whole point of the mesoscopic description is that a *finite* population carries multiplicative ensemble fluctuations with no analogue at either level the field usually works at — not in the individual stochastic oscillator (marginal), not in the deterministic thermodynamic-limit Ott–Antonsen reduction (which averages them away). The closed expressions for the stochastic order parameter quantify exactly the excess structure of the composite (finite-N ensemble) over its components and its N→∞ limit.
- **Stability**: The synchronization transition's critical exponents are extracted from the fluctuation corrections — i.e. the robust scaling behavior of the order parameter near criticality survives (and is corrected by) finite-size noise. Weak machine here: stability of the transition phenomenology under the perturbation "make N finite and the units noisy."

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- First closed-form expressions for the stochastic Kuramoto order parameter at finite N; the Ott–Antonsen ansatz is demoted from framework to approximation, with the missing piece identified precisely as multiplicative (not additive) ensemble noise.
- The mesoscopic bridge itself — a systematic layer between microscopic Langevin oscillators and the macroscopic Fokker–Planck/wigner picture — is a modeling contribution about *levels of description*, which the six machines describe but do not supply.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Ott–Antonsen ansatz lineage, stochastic Kuramoto literature, critical phenomena / finite-size scaling. No TDA, QEC, OT, or ML.
