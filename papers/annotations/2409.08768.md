## 2409.08768 --- Botvinick-Greenhouse, Oprea, Maulik, Yang (2024)
**"Measure-Theoretic Time-Delay Embedding"**
arXiv: 2409.08768 | math.DS/cs.LG

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems, optimal transport

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: The central move is recasting Takens' delay embedding as an optimal-transport problem. Classically the embedding Φ(x) = (h(x), h(ϕ(x)), …, h(ϕ^{2m}(x))) is a pointwise map on states; here the system is described Eulerian-style by a probability measure μ_t on state space evolving under the flow, and reconstruction becomes a pushforward Φ_#: P(M) → P(R^{2m+1}) between spaces of probability measures. The "assignment" being solved is not point-to-point but measure-to-measure: which coupling between observed-delay measures and full-state measures is induced by the dynamics. The computational procedure reconstructs the full state from time-lagged partial observations by solving this measure-level correspondence, using recent OT machinery (per the abstract) for the coupling.
- **Stability**: The stated design goal is robustness of the reconstruction to sparse and noisy observations — exactly where the classical theorem (noise-free observations, deterministic flow) breaks. The measure-theoretic framing is what buys the slack: individual trajectories need not be recovered exactly, only the pushforward image of the invariant/evolving measure, which degrades continuously under perturbation of the observed measure. (Depth-limited: abstract asserts robustness empirically across Lorenz-63, NOAA SST, ERA5; no perturbation-theoretic bound visible at abstract level.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The Eulerian reformulation itself: replacing the deterministic, noise-free, pointwise Takens setting with a measure-dynamics setting where the object reconstructed is a distribution over states, not a trajectory. The six machines describe the resulting correspondence, but the choice of Eulerian vs Lagrangian description is an orthogonal modeling commitment neither Matching nor Stability encodes.
- A concrete answer to the atlas's thinnest-cell question (Dynamics × Matching): the bridge runs in the direction Matching←Dynamics (dynamics supplies the coupling), via pushforward maps rather than cost matrices.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Classical Takens (1981) and its fractal/probabilistic refinements (Sauer–Yorke–Casdagli line); recent optimal-transport theory as the enabling toolkit; applications to climate reanalysis (NOAA, ERA5). No acknowledgment of the TDA persistence or QEC literatures.
