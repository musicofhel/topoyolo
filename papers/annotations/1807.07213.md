## 1807.07213 --- Moosavi, Montakhab & Valizadeh (2018)
**"Coexistence of scale invariant and rhythmic behavior in self-organized criticality"**
arXiv: 1807.07213 | cond-mat.dis-nn

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics, neuroscience

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The affirmative-mechanism half of the oscillation-criticality question: scale-free avalanche statistics of the driven Zhang model are shown to be robust under an oscillatory perturbation of the local threshold condition — rhythms embed INSIDE well-defined avalanches with size/duration scaling intact. The invariant that survives perturbation is the scaling class itself.
- **Parameterized homology**: Oscillation frequency as the parameter axis: average induced amplitude decreases monotonically with driving frequency, tracking the observed brain rhythm hierarchy — a family of perturbed models indexed by frequency, with the response profile traced across it.
- **Null hypothesis**: The paper's implicit null is the "theoretically incompatible" reading — criticality and oscillation as mutually exclusive dynamical motifs. The result is constructed against exactly that null: coexistence is demonstrated inside one model rather than argued.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Resonance-at-criticality as a functional claim: optimal amplification of the oscillatory drive occurs AT the critical point, so criticality is recast not merely as a state to detect but as the configuration where the system is maximally responsive to rhythmic input — an amplifier semantics for SOC that neither the Plenz-style evidence program nor the Touboul-Destexhe null program carries.
- Reconciliation mechanism for the batch's central tension: within the structured disagreement (2102.09124 vs 0910.0805/1203.0738), this entry supplies what both sides lack — a generative model in which the Plenz observation (avalanches embedded in nested oscillations) and the null camp's demand for mechanistic care are simultaneously satisfiable, without adjudicating the empirical question.
- The Zhang-model route keeps the result at the level of known-SOC dynamics: unlike thresholded-stochastic nulls (0910.0805), the underlying scale-free behavior here is genuine, so the oscillatory modulation rides on real criticality — a positive-control complement to the null lineage.
- What does NOT reduce: no distributional test discipline, no surrogate construction — the paper demonstrates existence in a model, not evidence procedure for data.
