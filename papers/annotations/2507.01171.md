## 2507.01171 --- Chambers & Meng (2025)
**"A Stable and Theoretically Grounded Gromov-Wasserstein Distance for Reeb Graph Comparison using Persistence Images"**
arXiv: 2507.01171 | cs.CG

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: topological significance weighting comes from extended persistence diagrams of the scalar field — the persistence lifecycle (ordinary / relative / extended-pairing) of sublevel-set structure is computed and then converted to persistence images, i.e. the paper consumes a filtration's birth-death summary as input to a matching functional.

- **Matching**: a Gromov-Wasserstein distance between Reeb graphs, viewed as metric measure spaces — relational (internal-distance) matching rather than node-attribute comparison. The GW coupling carries the graph-to-graph assignment.

- **Stability**: the headline theorem: the proposed "Reeb Gromov-Wasserstein" distance is stable under perturbations of the underlying scalar field — small function change implies small distance change between the induced Reeb graphs. This closes the data→structure→matching pipeline with an explicit ε–δ link at every stage.

- **Null hypothesis** (weak): the symmetric Reeb-radius variant plus persistence-image weighting acts as a significance filter — branches whose persistence mass vanishes contribute vanishing weight, so noise-level features are discounted inside the metric itself.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Composition of three machines into one proven-stable pipeline: filtration summary (extended persistence) → probability weighting (persistence images) → relational matching (GW), with stability carried end-to-end from scalar-field perturbation to final distance.
2. Persistence images used as a *measure-defining* device for GW (the mm-space's probability measure is built from persistence), not merely as a vectorized feature for a classifier — a structural bridge between the Parameterized-homology and Matching machines that the atlas has few instances of.
3. Computational motivation: existing exact Reeb-graph metrics (e.g. interleaving-type distances) are prohibitive; this trades exactness of bottleneck-style bounds for tractable GW computation while keeping a proof.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Reeb-graph comparison literature, extended persistence (Agarwal et al. lineage), GW distances on mm-spaces. No cross-domain engagement beyond computational topology/geometry.
