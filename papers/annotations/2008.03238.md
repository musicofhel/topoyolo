## 2008.03238 --- Ramstead, Hesp, Tschantz, Smith, Constant & Friston (2020)
**"Neural and phenotypic representation under the free-energy principle"**
arXiv: 2008.03238 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The dual-aspect information geometry is the excess made geometric: given Markov-blanket partitioning, internal-state trajectories acquire an *intrinsic* geometry (their own state-space shape) AND an *extrinsic* geometry (parameters of beliefs about external states). The representational content is structure in the joint internal-external system that no internal marginal carries by fiat — it emerges from blanket-mediated coupling at NESS.
- **Stability**: Representation is claimed to be stable precisely because it rides on nonequilibrium steady-state dynamics: self-organizing ensembles that share a generative model keep encoding recoverable stimulus information — persistence of encoding under the ensemble's own dynamics is the stability certificate offered.
- **Null hypothesis**: The neuronal packet simulations carry an implicit null — ensembles WITHOUT the right shared probabilistic generative model should fail to encode recoverable information; recoverability above chance is what the Markovian-structure-plus-NNESS hypothesis must earn.
- **Parameterized homology**: Weak — the blanket hierarchy over spatiotemporal scales recurs here as the substrate on which distributed (population-code) representation sits.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The intrinsic/extrinsic geometry split: two information geometries on the same internal states simultaneously — one descriptive of trajectory, one semantic about the world. Neither machine alone predicts the doubling.
- The neuronal packet hypothesis: representation emerging automatically in blanket-bound neural ensembles, with simulations as demonstration rather than proof.
- Lineage position: this is the constructive successor to 1906.10184 written AFTER Biehl et al.'s critique (2001.06408) was available — it re-founds representation on blanket *structure* rather than the disproven lemma, which is exactly the repair path the critique left open ("the general ideas are worth pursuing"). Whether its formulation uses an equivalence-class-safe blanket definition is not checkable from the abstract — flagged for full-depth follow-up.
