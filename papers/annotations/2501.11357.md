## 2501.11357 --- Fadera (2025)
**"On the dimension of pullback attractors in recurrent neural networks"**
arXiv: 2501.11357 | math.DS

**Domain(s)**: dynamical systems

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The paper bounds the box-counting dimension of the reservoir computer's pullback attractor — the invariant subset of state space that training and prediction phases approximate — by the dimension of the input-sequence space. This is a stability-of-complexity result: the effective dynamics a high-dimensional reservoir actually supports cannot exceed what the drive carries, so the reconstruction correspondence is stable against the reservoir's own excess state-space dimension. Non-autonomous (pullback) machinery is what makes the bound hold under time-dependent driving.
- **Matching** (weak): The result is the dimension-theoretic half of the embedding conjecture for general (nonlinear activation) reservoir systems — i.e. it constrains the generalized-synchronization correspondence of `annotations/2108.05024.md` without constructing it. (Depth-limited: abstract proves an upper bound; existence of the embedding itself remains open at abstract level.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The resolution of the apparent paradox that a very-high-dimensional recurrent network can faithfully learn low-dimensional chaotic dynamics: the pullback-attractor dimension bound shows the reservoir exhibits *effective* low-dimensional dynamics regardless of its nominal state dimension. None of the six machines speaks about dimension bookkeeping between coupled systems; this is an orthogonal constraint on where matchings can live.
- First non-autonomous-dynamical-systems treatment of reservoir computing's success conditions, extending the linear-activation case (`annotations/2108.05024.md`) toward general activations via inequality rather than conjugacy.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Reservoir computing; Takens embedding conjecture for reservoir computers (Grigoryeva–Hart–Ortega line); fractal dimension / Lyapunov invariants of reconstructed attractors. No acknowledgment of TDA or OT literatures.
