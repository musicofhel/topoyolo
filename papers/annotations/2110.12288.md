## 2110.12288 --- Glad & Woolf (2021)
**"Path Signature Area-Based Causal Discovery in Coupled Time Series"**
arXiv: 2110.12288 | stat.ML

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching**: The signed area between two streams is the antisymmetric part of the degree-2 path-signature term — an iterated-integral pairing of the two series' increments. Lag/lead detection pairs time-shifted versions of the series and matches which shift maximizes the signed-area magnitude; causal direction becomes a matching problem over the lag axis.
- **Stability**: Confidence sequences give anytime-valid, sequentially tightening significance for the signed-area magnitude — a formal robustness certificate for the inferred link that converges as sampling length grows (the paper's main methodological import beyond the statistic itself).
- **Null hypothesis**: The test is defined against the null "no directed coupling": under it the pairwise signed areas across time shifts stay within the confidence-sequence boundary; a crossing at nonzero lag certifies a lag/lead relationship.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Importing rough-path/path-signature algebra as the feature extractor for causal discovery — a new algebraic object on the corpus's CCM shelf. Where CCM matches reconstructed state vectors, this matches iterated integrals: second-order joint increments rather than pointwise states.
- Anytime-valid inference (confidence sequences) attached to a causal-discovery statistic — the significance machinery is arguably the more novel half relative to GC/CCM/PCMCI baselines named in the abstract.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Names Granger causality, CCM, and PCMCI as the existing toolbox it complements. No Rosetta-cross-domain awareness; path signatures enter from stochastic-analysis/ML (rough paths) with no citation bridge to topology.
