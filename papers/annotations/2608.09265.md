## 2608.09265 --- Yachimura & Zou (2026)
**"Entropic Partial Optimal Transport and Partial Gromov--Wasserstein Distance between Gaussian Mixtures"**
arXiv: 2608.09265 | math.OC

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: a partial Gromov-Wasserstein distance between mixtures, after identifying each Gaussian mixture with a finite mm-space of components — relational matching with the mass-conservation constraint *relaxed* (partial coupling: not all mass must move). Entropic regularization supplies existence and uniqueness of the finite minimizer.

- **Stability**: quantitative large-penalty estimates (entropic partial solution → unregularized partial solution as penalty weight grows) plus the zero-entropy limit — continuity of the relaxation path. The metric property of the partial mixture GW distance is itself the stability-type statement that the functional separates objects.

- **Null hypothesis** (weak): the large-penalty / zero-entropy limits are controlled degenerations of the regularized problem — reference cases where the relaxed machinery provably collapses back onto the exact problem.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Mass-conservation relaxation carried into the Matching machine's relational (GW) setting for continuous parametric families: balanced GW forces total overlap; the partial formulation prices outliers, missing parts, and partial overlap — an explicit "match only what matches" semantics.
2. A two-limit analysis (large-penalty → unregularized; entropy → 0) giving a commutative diagram of relaxations over the same coupling family, with displacement interpolations and barycentric projections constructed along the way.
3. No chain complex, parameterized homology, or joint-vs-marginal content; experiments are validation on synthetic mixtures/point clouds.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: entropic OT (Sinkhorn lineage), unbalanced/partial OT literature, GW distances on mm-spaces. No cross-domain engagement.
