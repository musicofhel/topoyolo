## 2212.14123 --- Mémoli & Needham (2022)
**"Comparison Results for Gromov-Wasserstein and Gromov-Monge Distances"**
arXiv: 2212.14123 | math.MG

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: The paper's whole subject is a split *inside* the Matching machine that the atlas has so far treated as one: GW distances (couplings/soft assignment, Kantorovich-style) vs Gromov-Monge distances (maps/deterministic assignment, Monge-style) on metric measure spaces. Both are optimal assignments between internal-distance structures.

- **Stability**: Bi-Hölder equivalence between GM distance and an isometry-invariant Monge OT distance used in shape/image analysis — a quantitative two-sided control transferring robustness between the coupling and map formulations of the machine.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Kantorovich-vs-Monge resolved *within* relational matching: GM = GW exactly for non-atomic metric measure spaces — i.e. the deterministic-assignment restriction costs nothing once atoms vanish. This is a structural fact about the Matching machine itself (when does soft matching collapse to hard matching), not an instance of any of the six machines.
2. Variants including a Monge version of Sturm's L^p transportion distance, with precise comparisons — a calibration table for choosing which instantiation of the machine a given application actually needs.
3. Nothing here touches chain complex, parameterized homology, joint-vs-marginal excess, or null hypothesis; it is internal foundations for one machine.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Explicitly framed as inspired by Kantorovich's relaxation of Monge's original OT formulation — the authors themselves carry the OT ancestry into the relational setting, unlike most corpus entries where the bridge is reconstructed by the annotator. No TDA/inference engagement.
