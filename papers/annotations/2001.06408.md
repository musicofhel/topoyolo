## 2001.06408 --- Biehl, Pollock & Kanai (2020)
**"A Technical Critique of Some Parts of the Free Energy Principle"**
arXiv: 2001.06408 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Null hypothesis**: The paper IS a constructed null against 1906.10184: it proves by counterexample that the original free energy lemma is false as stated, shows the blanket-rewriting steps in the equations of motion require previously unstated assumptions, and demonstrates that the several published "Markov blanket" definitions are non-equivalent — i.e. "the FEP derivation goes through" fails under explicitly exhibited systems.
- **Stability**: The critique locates where the stability claim loses its certificate: even when the free energy lemma does hold, it forces equality of variational density and ergodic conditional density — so the Bayesian-inference interpretation of internal states rests on an unjustified equality rather than on robustness proven from the dynamics.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: Indirect but structural — by showing the blanket definitions are non-equivalent across Friston's works, the paper attacks the partition that generates the joint-vs-marginal reading; if the boundary itself shifts between formulations, the excess it licenses is not a well-defined quantity.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The counterexample to the free energy lemma: not a philosophical objection but a mathematical disproof of the corpus-side claim paper's central step.
- The definitional audit: multiple inequivalent Markov-blanket definitions circulating in the same lineage — a formalism whose composite-boundary object is version-dependent.
- The parameterization drift finding: newer FEP formulations parameterize variational densities by different variables than older works, changing the theory's interpretation while keeping its vocabulary — an antisynonym-generating mechanism internal to one lineage.
- What does NOT reduce (and is conceded): the authors state these problems do not rule out that the FEP's general ideas are worth pursuing — this is a refutation of derivations, not of the program; the null is partial, like 1906.05212 against 1410.3831.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Directly and solely engaged with the Friston lineage (FEP papers, generalized coordinates/filtering).
