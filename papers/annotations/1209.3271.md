## 1209.3271 --- Girardi-Schappo, Kinouchi & Tragtenberg (2012)
**"Critical Avalanches and Subsampling in Map-based Neural Networks"**
arXiv: 1209.3271 | cond-mat.dis-nn

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Null hypothesis**: Two nulls, both aimed at the experimental SOC program: (1) a generative alternative — synaptic noise (a uniform noise term in the synaptic coupling of map-based neurons) is sufficient to drive critical avalanches, so "self-organization" need not be the mechanism; noise-driven criticality is the reference the self-organized reading must beat. (2) The subsampling analysis — measuring avalanches from a sampled subset of units, as every in vivo multi-electrode study must — is shown to shape apparent scaling, making subsampling the measurement-artifact channel for SOC claims.
- **Stability**: The critical avalanche regime is located by neuronal/synaptic parameter choices (excitatory-by-rebound with fast synapses), and its persistence under subsampling is precisely what is probed — which scaling features survive reduction of the observed unit set.
- **Parameterized homology**: Weakly, the parameter space of synaptic properties (speed, noise strength, neuron class) swept to find where the critical state appears.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Mechanism pluralism for neural avalanches: synaptic noise as the driver places the phenomenon closer to noise-induced criticality than to the homeostatic self-organization story of 2102.09124 — an alternative mechanism whose existence weakens any inference from observed scaling back to adaptive self-tuning.
- Explicit subsampling methodology on model data: the paper turns the practical necessity of partial recording into an object of study — directly anticipating the empirical-null camp's measurement-channel critique (1203.0738's pLFP-vs-units control is the experimental cousin of this simulation-side result).
- Map-based modeling discipline: dynamical properties (spike shape, excitability, PSP summation) emerge from the maps rather than being imposed — the model earns its avalanches without hard-wiring them.
- What does NOT reduce: no surrogate statistics, no distribution-family testing — it supplies generative mechanisms and artifact channels, not evidence procedure.
