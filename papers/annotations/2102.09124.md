## 2102.09124 --- Plenz, Ribeiro, Miller, Kells, Vakili & Capek (2021)
**"Self-Organized Criticality in the Brain"**
arXiv: 2102.09124 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The core machine read as a stability claim: layered cortex self-organizes TOWARD a second-order phase transition and holds there — branching parameter ≈ 1 maintained homeostatically, with E/I balance and dopamine as control parameters whose perturbation moves the system off criticality; the −3/2 size-distribution slope is the invariant the dynamics returns to.
- **Parameterized homology**: The dynamical repertoire indexed by preparation state: organotypic cultures/acute slices show the full critical motif set (up-states, nested theta/gamma oscillations embedding avalanches, coherence potentials), while layer-less dissociated cultures show first-order-transition-like dynamics — structure of the medium as the parameter along which the transition class changes.
- **Null hypothesis**: The paper's implicit nulls are the subcritical/supercritical regimes (branching ≠ 1) and the dissociated-culture control; its scaling evidence, however, is regression-shape class (log-log power laws), which is exactly the channel the paired nulls indict.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The multi-motif coexistence claim: avalanches are not free-standing but embedded in up-states and nested oscillations in superficial layers — criticality as one component of a coordinated dynamical repertoire rather than a bare exponent.
- Control-parameter identification for a biological SOC system: E/I balance and dopamine as experimentally manipulable handles on the approach to the branching = 1 fixed point.
- The preparation contrast (layered vs dissociated cultures mapping to second- vs first-order-like dynamics) — an experimental parameter axis for transition-class inference.
- What does NOT reduce: no surrogate construction, no distributional test discipline — the paper asserts scale invariance from regression slopes and does not instantiate the null machinery its opponents use; that gap IS the disagreement's shape.
