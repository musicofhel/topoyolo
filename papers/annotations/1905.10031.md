## 1905.10031 --- Jain, Koehler, Liu & Mossel (2019)
**"Accuracy-Memory Tradeoffs and Phase Transitions in Belief Propagation"**
arXiv: 1905.10031 | cs.IT/math.ST

**Domain(s)**: Information theory, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: The proof "combines in novel fashion tools from recursive reconstruction, information theory, and optimal transport": optimal transport enters as the device for bounding the accuracy of message-passing iterates — comparing distributions of messages via a transport cost is what converts memory bounds into accuracy bounds near the critical point. The matching machine appears instrumentally, inside the converse argument rather than as the object studied.

- **Stability**: The core result is a phase-transition location: any bounded-memory recursive algorithm on tree reconstruction with the BSC has its accuracy phase transition strictly below the Kesten–Stigum (BP) threshold — proving the Evans–Kenyon–Peres–Schulman conjecture. The KS threshold is characterized as a stability boundary for belief propagation itself, and an asymptotic-normality result for BP and other message-passing algorithms near the critical point pins down behavior at the boundary.

- **Null hypothesis**: Bounded-memory message passing functions as the null algorithmic class: BP's superiority over *any* member of the class quantifies how much structure the unrestricted matcher exploits that no resource-bounded matcher can.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. A memory-accuracy tradeoff theorem: the gap between the bounded-memory transition and the KS threshold is a quantitative statement that reconstruction structure beyond the threshold exists but is inaccessible to constrained algorithms — a computational-to-statistical gap proved rather than conjectured.
2. Optimal transport used as a proof technology for a phase-transition result (rare direction of import: an OT-flavored tool imported into the cavity/reconstruction setting, not the reverse).
3. Asymptotic normality of message-passing iterates at criticality.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Evans–Kenyon–Peres–Schulman (2000) conjecture; community detection and phylogenetic reconstruction applications; cavity method of statistical physics; Kesten–Stigum bound. Explicit cross-domain awareness (info theory ↔ statphys ↔ phylogenetics).
