## 2209.00527 --- Ito (2022)
**"Geometric thermodynamics for the Fokker-Planck equation: Stochastic thermodynamic links between information geometry and optimal transport"**
arXiv: 2209.00527 | cond-mat.stat-mech/math-ph

**Domain(s)**: Statistical physics, optimal transport, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Matching** (core): The paper's stated program is exactly a bridge between the Matching geometry and information geometry, both hosted on the space of probability densities: the gradient-flow expression of the Fokker–Planck equation ties excess entropy production rate to information geometry, and the OT velocity field is read through information geometry in the space of *path* probability densities. Unlike candidate-05 (2103.00503), where Wasserstein geometry appears only inside a lower bound, here the OT structure itself (Benamou–Brenier-style velocity fields) is one of the two legs being identified.
- **Joint-vs-marginal** (weak): One of the advertised outputs is a decomposition of the entropy production rate for non-equilibrium systems — dissipation split into attributable components — which is the composite-systems move in thermodynamic units. (Depth-limited at abstract level; candidate-07 1810.09545 makes the additivity story explicit.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- Trade-off relations between thermodynamic cost and fluctuation of an observable: a constraint family coupling dissipation to observable variance that neither machine encodes alone.
- Optimal protocols for minimum thermodynamic cost derived inside the unified geometric frame — the "physical cost of driving a coupling" problem again, now with an information-geometric dual.
- Bridge value: with 2103.00503 this completes a small Ito-lineage cluster showing that the atlas's Matching machine and its joint-vs-marginal decomposition machine are not merely analogous in stochastic thermodynamics but *interderivable* via excess entropy production — the strongest evidence so far that the two machines share more than vocabulary.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Information geometry (Amari lineage); optimal transport theory; stochastic thermodynamics (Fokker–Planck, Seifert lineage); the author's own earlier geometrical results. No acknowledgment of TDA, QEC, or neuroscience.
