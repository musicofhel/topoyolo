## 2012.02634 --- Perez (2020)
**"On C0-persistent homology and trees"**
arXiv: 2012.02634 | math.AT

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: A metric tree construction is built whose superlevel-set connected components correctly identify those of any R-valued continuous function on X, and the H0-persistence diagram is retrievable from the tree — H0 persistence re-derived as an invariant of a metric object rather than of a filtered complex.

- **Stability**: A quantitative version of the Wasserstein stability theorem is proven for sufficiently regular X and α-Hölder functions: the persistence diagram depends Hölder-continuously on the C0 perturbation of the function. This is the analytic-function refinement of the barcode-stability machine (control-theoretic robustness under perturbation, with explicit regularity hypotheses).

- **Matching**: Weak substrate only — the Wasserstein distances appearing in the stability theorem are the p-matching distances between diagrams; no independent matching contribution.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Tree-as-invariant: the merge-tree/Reeb-style construction is shown to be a faithful carrier of H0 persistence, connecting the one-dimensional invariant to the diagram without passing through a filtration of complexes.
2. Homological dimension bounds: Schweinhart's homological dimension bounded in terms of the upper-box dimension of X — a metric-regularity ⇒ topological-complexity bound partially answering Schweinhart's question.
3. Random-field application: superlevel-set topology of random fields treated with these quantitative tools (statistical-physics adjacent, not developed here).

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Schweinhart's homological dimension; classical Wasserstein stability (Cohen-Steiner–Edelsbrunner–Harer lineage). No cross-domain engagement.
