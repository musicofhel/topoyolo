## quant-ph/0702059 --- Plenio & Virmani (2007)
**"Spin chains and channels with memory"**
arXiv: quant-ph/0702059 | quant-ph

**Domain(s)**: Quantum, statistical physics, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: The paper's program is to treat channel capacity under correlated error as a function of a memory/correlation parameter and to locate non-analytic points — capacities that display 'phase transitions' as the error-correlation strength varies. Capacity curves with kinks at critical correlation values are exactly invariant-across-parameter-family structure.

- **Null hypothesis**: Independent-error channels are the destroyed-structure reference: removing the correlations between channel uses is precisely "destroy the structure, preserve everything else" (same marginal error rates, no memory). The paper asks when capacity behavior of the correlated channel differs qualitatively from this i.i.d. reference.

- **Stability**: Mapping correlated-error channel models onto spin-chain Hamiltonians imports stability machinery: whether capacity is continuous or jumps in the memory parameter mirrors how the associated many-body system responds to perturbation of its coupling; the correspondence gives qualitative robustness criteria for capacity statements under model changes.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. The explicit dictionary: quantum channel capacity with correlated errors ↔ critical phenomena in many-body physics — including exactly solvable models where the correspondence is complete rather than analogical.
2. Demonstration that capacity non-analyticities ('phase transitions') are real features of memory-channel models, not artifacts — legitimizing capacity-as-order-parameter reasoning.
3. Method transfer in both directions: many-body techniques solve channel models; channel questions pick out physically interesting spin-chain regimes.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Earlier work on additive capacity bounds with correlated errors, spin-chain thermodynamics, and the capacity formalism of Bennett–Shor–Smolin–Thauland for channels with memory. Explicit QEC↔StatPhys bridge; no TDA/OT content.
