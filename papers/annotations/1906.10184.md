## 1906.10184 --- Friston (2019)
**"A free energy principle for a particular physics"**
arXiv: 1906.10184 | q-bio.NC

**Domain(s)**: neuroscience, statistical physics

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The Markov blanket is the paper's core composite-system object: internal (x) and external (η) states are rendered conditionally independent given blanket (sensory + active) states, so any structure the internal states "contain" about the external ones is precisely a joint-vs-marginal excess — correlation across the blanket that no marginal alone carries. The free energy functional is then the bookkeeping device for that excess at nonequilibrium steady state.
- **Null hypothesis**: "Things that can be distinguished from other things" are defined by statistical independence — the null for thinghood is the destruction of the blanket (breaking conditional independence dissolves the particle). Solenoid/flow-box conditions and fluctuation themmas are deployed to show when self-organization to NESS is *not* generic.
- **Stability**: Self-organization to nonequilibrium steady-state is the stability claim: internal states stay in a restricted set over long times despite external perturbation; the attractor's existence is what licenses reading internal dynamics as inference. Pullback attractors and itô/stratonovich stochastic differential equations supply the perturbation framework.
- **Parameterized homology**: Recursive composition of Markov blankets at increasing spatiotemporal scales — each nesting level is a parameter value of the decomposition, with ensembles-of-things as the objects tracked across levels.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The scope claim itself: one formalism intended to span quantum → statistical → classical mechanics via blanket-mediated conditional independence, i.e. joint-vs-marginal elevated from an analysis tool to an ontology of "things."
- The inference-as-physics move: internal-state information geometry is *interpreted* as belief about external states — representation read off from nonequilibrium steady-state dynamics rather than posited.
- What does NOT reduce: the derivation's load-bearing steps (the free energy lemma equating nonequilibrium steady-state density with variational density) are exactly what the paired critique shows fail without unstated assumptions — see `annotations/2001.06408.md`. The corpus position is the pair, not this monograph alone.
