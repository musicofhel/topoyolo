## 1408.1224 --- Barato, Seifert (2014)
**"Stochastic thermodynamics with information reservoirs"**
arXiv: 1408.1224 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: Statistical physics, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal** (core): The whole construction is a composite of a physical system plus an *information reservoir* (a sequence of bits). The generalized second law is precisely a joint-vs-marginal statement: entropy production of the joint system–reservoir dynamics obeys the standard inequality only after crediting the information flow — work extraction from a single heat bath becomes possible exactly because the marginal bookkeeping on the system alone is the wrong ledger. The "information processing entropy production" is the excess term.
- **Null hypothesis** (weak): The fluctuation theorem and the derived inequality function as the corrected baseline against which apparent second-law violations are judged — i.e., what looks like a null-model violation (entropy decreasing under feedback) is restored to significance by adding the reservoir's contribution. (Depth-limited at abstract level.)

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- A *systematic linear response theory for information-processing machines*: efficiency at maximum power for a unicyclic machine powered by an information reservoir can deviate from the universal value 1/2 — thermodynamic universality classes break when the driving is informational rather than energetic. This couples the atlas's machines to a regime none of them encode: engines whose fuel is correlation.
- The erasure-limited case (efficiency at max erasure rate = 1/2) gives a Landauer-cost boundary condition tying the reservoir's bit-sequence model back to physical dissipation.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Stochastic thermodynamics (Seifert lineage); information thermodynamics (feedback/Sagawa–Ueda line implicitly); Landauer erasure. No acknowledgment of TDA, QEC, or neuroscience — though the bit-reservoir is structurally the same object QEC calls a classical side channel.
