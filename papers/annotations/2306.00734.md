## 2306.00734 --- Gutknecht, Makkeh & Wibral (2023)
**"From Babel to Boole: The Logical Organization of Information Decompositions"**
arXiv: 2306.00734 | cs.IT

**Domain(s)**: information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Chain complex**: Recasts the zoo of PID base-concepts as logical conditions on parthood relations between decomposition components and mutual-information terms — a formal-language layer sitting above the Williams-Beer lattice, showing all published base-concepts (and novel ones) instantiate ONE general pattern. The unification-by-single-algebraic-skeleton move is the chain-complex machine applied to the meta-level of decompositions themselves.
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: Redundancy, synergy, unique and union information are precisely the different ways joint-level information relates to its marginals; the paper shows choosing a base-concept is choosing *which* joint-vs-marginal direction you take as primitive, with the others derived.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The mereological formulation itself: base-concepts as formal-logic conditions on parthood, replacing measure-first convention. A general pattern of logical conditions of which every literature base-concept is a special case — plus one previously unknown special case ("vulnerable information").
- Meta-result relevant to the atlas: the PID families differ by choice of logical primitive, not by underlying lattice — i.e. "same machine, different axioms" made precise inside one domain.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Their own prior mereological PID work; the redundancy-based (Williams-Beer), synergy-, unique- (Bertschinger et al.) and union-based traditions. Siloed in information theory. Abstract-only provenance — depth-limited.
