## 2109.08918 --- Song, Choi & Kahng (2021)
**"Machine learning approaches for Kuramoto coupled oscillator systems"**
arXiv: 2109.08918 | cond-mat.stat-mech

**Domain(s)**: statistical physics; dynamical systems

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Stability**: The core deliverable is a machine-learned determination of the transition point and criticality of a *hybrid* synchronization transition — locating where the ordered state is stable as a function of coupling, and extracting the critical behavior, by treating learned order parameters as transition detectors rather than deriving them analytically.
- **Matching** (weak): The network-structure-inference result recovers the coupling graph from chaotic trajectory patterns — an inverse identification of the composite's structure from its dynamics, same inference direction as the reservoir-computing / generalized-synchronization cluster (`2108.05024`, `2108.04074`), here with the reconstructed object being the network itself.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- A hybrid synchronization transition (simultaneous first- and second-order character) whose criticality is pinned by ML rather than by ansatz-based analysis.
- Structure-from-chaos: using chaotic collective patterns as a sufficient probe of network topology, with an explicit forward look to EEG-style inverse problems.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Kuramoto transition literature, ML-for-classical-systems, EEG/neural-structure inference as outlook. No TDA, QEC, or OT.
