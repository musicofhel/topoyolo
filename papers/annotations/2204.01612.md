## 2204.01612 --- Lei, Hassani & Saeedi Bidokhti (2022)
**"Neural Estimation of the Rate-Distortion Function With Applications to Operational Source Coding"**
arXiv: 2204.01612 | cs.IT/cs.LG

**Domain(s)**: Information theory, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Matching**: The rate-distortion functional is the constrained soft-matching of the Blahut–Arimoto lineage (see `blahut-arimoto-1972.md`): minimize I(X;X̂) over conditional distributions P(x̂|x) subject to E[d(X,X̂)] ≤ D. NERD's contribution is to re-formulate that functional optimization so a neural network can amortize it in regimes where the alternating-minimization matching update is computationally infeasible (continuous, high-dimensional alphabets). The estimator returns the rate-distortion-achieving channel P*(x̂|x) itself — i.e. the optimal soft assignment — plus samples from its output marginal.

- **Null hypothesis**: The operational point of the paper is explicitly null-hypothesis machinery: R(D) is the theoretical optimum against which real DNN compressors are tested, and NERD supplies the null's value on data where it was previously uncomputable. The finding "DNN codecs are within several bits of R(D) on real images" is a null-comparison result — the gap between achieved performance and the ideal matcher bounds how much structure remains unexploited.

- **Parameterized homology** (weak): The estimated object is the whole curve R(D), traced as the distortion parameter varies; each D selects a different optimal test channel, exactly the parameterized-family picture of the foundational annotation.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Variational re-formulation of the RD objective that makes it amenable to neural functional optimization (parametric D_θ vs the exact BA fixed-point iteration).
2. Operational payoff via reverse channel coding: the estimated achieving channel converts into a one-shot lossy code with guaranteed rate and distortion — the estimator is not just diagnostic but constructive.
3. Evidence that near-optimal lossy compression is achievable on natural images without computing empirical distributions.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Blahut–Arimoto (as the computationally infeasible baseline being replaced), reverse channel coding (Li & El Gamal line), DNN codec literature.
