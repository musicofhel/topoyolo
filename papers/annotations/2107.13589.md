## 2107.13589 — Pattison, Beverland, da Silva & Delfosse (2021)
**"Improved quantum error correction using soft information"**

**Domain(s)**: Quantum error correction, information theory

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Joint-vs-marginal excess**: The core result is literally a joint-over-marginal gain in decoder form. Hard decoders keep only the binary marginal of the measurement outcome distribution (a continuous current or photon count collapsed to ±1); soft decoders decode from the full joint distribution of analog outcomes. The measured 25% threshold gain IS the excess of joint information over its binary marginal, cashed out as logical performance.
- **Matching**: Soft MWPM — edge weights derived from likelihood ratios of the continuous measurement record rather than hard failure probabilities; the matching machine survives the refinement intact, only its cost function is promoted from marginal-derived to joint-derived.
- **Phase transitions**: Error threshold raised 25% above any hard decoder under phenomenological Gaussian noise — the critical error rate itself moves when the decoder's information access improves.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The soft measurement error model with amplitude damping: measurement time induces a trade-off between resolution (more time → better soft information) and disturbance (more time → more additional decoherence of the qubits). Neither machine alone captures this two-sided cost.
- The sensitivity result: distance-19 surface code, five-fold increase in measurement time → thousand-fold increase in logical error rate; and the physical-error-rate-optimal measurement time differs from the logical-performance-optimal one. Joint optimization of the physical and QEC layers is forced by the mismatch — a concrete case where layer-wise (marginal) optimization loses to joint optimization, mirroring the paper's own soft-information theme one level up.
- Union-Find also modified to consume soft information — showing the gain is decoder-architecture-independent within the fast-decoder class.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Standard MWPM and Union-Find decoders; experimental measurement records (continuous currents, photon counts). Abstract-only provenance — depth-limited.
