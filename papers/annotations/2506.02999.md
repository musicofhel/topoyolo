## 2506.02999 --- Broomhead & Pirashvili (2025)
**"An isometry theorem for persistent homology of circle-valued functions"**
arXiv: 2506.02999 | math.AT

**Domain(s)**: TDA

**Abstract machines instantiated**:

- **Parameterized homology**: Persistence modules over circle-valued functions — the index leaves the poset (ℝ, ≤) for S¹; barcodes generalised to arcs on a geometric model built from the derived category of quiver representations. The parameterized-homology machine shown to survive a non-poset (cyclic) index.

- **Matching**: The bottleneck distance is extended to the arc-barcodes, and the paper's ISOMETRY THEOREM equates it with the interleaving distance in this setting — the Matching↔Stability duality pair (1205.3669 + 2201.06650) sharpened from inequality/bounding to exact isometric identification.

- **Stability**: The extended interleaving distance on circle-valued modules is the perturbation metric; the isometry theorem transfers all stability statements to bottleneck form and conversely.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
1. Cyclic index: first-class extension of the interleaving/bottleneck apparatus beyond monotone (ℝ-indexed) filtrations to S¹-valued observables — relevant wherever the observable is angular (phase, periodic orbits).
2. Isometry (not merely inequality): interleaving = bottleneck exactly for this class, upgrading the usual d≤b-type relations across the atlas's two perturbation metrics to equality.
3. Derived-category geometric model for barcodes-as-arcs — categorical packaging parallel to Bubenik–Scott but for a different index shape.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Classical interleaving and bottleneck distances; derived categories of quiver representations. No cross-domain engagement.
