## 1211.1162 — Donges, Donner, Kurths (2012)
**"Testing time series irreversibility using complex network methods"**

**Domain(s)**: Dynamical systems

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Null hypothesis**: Time-reversal symmetry as the null — but reached WITHOUT surrogate generation. The reference distribution comes from statistically comparing time-directed variants of visibility-graph statistics (degree, local clustering), i.e. the null machinery is replaced by a direct two-sample comparison between the forward and reversed encodings. An explicit surrogate-free branch of the null lineage.
- **Joint-vs-marginal**: The test statistic lives on the directed graph encoding, not the marginal amplitude distribution: irreversibility appears as excess structure in the time-directed network measures that marginal statistics (histograms, spectra — all reversal-symmetric) cannot see.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- The nonlinear-map step: irreversible dynamics → asymmetric graph statistics via the visibility-graph transformation. Which statistic detects which asymmetry is an encoding question the machines don't cover.
- Applicability to short time series, where surrogate ensembles are too thin to rank against.

**Connections the authors acknowledge**: Explicitly positions against surrogate data testing as the incumbent method; visibility-graph literature (Lacasa et al.). Abstract-only provenance — depth-limited.
