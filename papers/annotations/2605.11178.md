## 2605.11178 --- Dönmez, Mosig, Fritsche & Koch (2026)
**"Oversmoothing as Representation Degeneracy in Neural Sheaf Diffusion"**
arXiv: 2605.11178 | cs.LG, math.RT

**Domain(s)**: TDA, machine learning

**Abstract machines instantiated**:
- **Chain complex**: Neural sheaf diffusion is the incidence-quiver representation of the cochain complex C⁰ → C¹; its diffusion limit is known to be the space of global sections (the harmonic/Hodge kernel H⁰). The paper decomposes that harmonic space along direct-sum decompositions of the underlying quiver representation — a Krull–Schmidt-style splitting of kernel data induced by splitting of boundary data.
- **Stability**: The core move: sheaf geometries are points in a finite-dimensional representation space, and oversmoothing is reframed as *degeneration* toward low-complexity summands whose global sections lose discriminative information. GIT (geometric invariant theory) stability and moment-map principles supply the formalism: moment-map-inspired regularizers bias learned restriction maps toward balanced (stable-point) geometries. This is stability in exactly the Rosetta sense — a robustness/instability theory of where the diffusion flow's fixed point lands.
- **Null hypothesis**: The equal-stalk obstruction plays a negative-result role: when all stalk dimensions coincide (d_v = d_e), admissibility of learnable stability parameters forces the trivial all-object summand onto a "stability wall" — a structural configuration where adaptive stability is vacuous. Non-uniform stalk dimensions are shown to remove it.

**What is genuinely new (not reducible to shared abstraction)**:
- First corpus entry to import Geometric Invariant Theory into the chain-complex machine: stability here is Mumford/GIT semistability of quiver representations, not persistence-module or Lipschitz robustness — a third, algebraic-geometric meaning of "stability," applied to the machine itself rather than to data passing through it.
- The diagnosis that oversmoothing — usually treated empirically in GNNs — has an exact representation-theoretic identity: degeneration of the learned coboundary toward unstable (direct-sum-decomposable, low-complexity) summands, with the harmonic limit degrading accordingly. A failure mode of learning becomes an orbit-closure statement.
